## Mathematical results

- Unconditional strict mean-center-distance bound corresponding to Graffiti.pc no. 268.
- Partial ceiling result (no. 269): odd total domination cardinality, and cardinality two in the absence of a four-cycle.
- Induced-center characterization for the dominating-edge class.
- Exact marked minimum appendage classification: 0, 2 or 4.
- Exact spectrum for m ≥ 2 central vertices when total domination number is two: 2 − j/binom(m,2), with m−1 ≤ j ≤ binom(m,2).
- Sharp maximum 2 − 2/m, with equality exactly for an induced center that is a tree.
- Every spectral value is attained on m+2 vertices; this order is minimal among realizations with a proper center.
